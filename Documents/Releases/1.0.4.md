iGhostVT 1.0.4 repeats held keys and no longer quits when the disk fills up.

- Holding a key on a hardware keyboard, or holding backspace on the software keyboard, typed one character and stopped. Held keys now repeat.
- When the device ran out of storage, iGhostVT quit on the next line it wrote to its log. It now skips the lines it cannot write and keeps running.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `xros-arm64e` for a jailbroken Apple Vision Pro, or `iGhostVT-1.0.4-macos.zip` for a Mac — the Mac app is ad-hoc signed, so clear its quarantine once with `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/iGhostVT.app`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
