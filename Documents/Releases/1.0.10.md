iGhostVT 1.0.10 selects text right in the terminal on iPhone and iPad, and makes tabs easier to rearrange on the Mac.

- Selecting text used to open a separate sheet with a copy of the screen. A long press now selects in the terminal itself, with handles to drag and the system's Copy menu.
- On the Mac, dragging a tab in the tab strip used to move or reorder it unpredictably. Now dragging a tab moves the window, as dragging a title bar does, and a long press picks the tab up to reorder it. Drag the tab out of the bar to open it in a new window, or drop it on another window's bar to move it there.
- On iPad, swiping the tab strip picked up a tab instead of scrolling. A swipe scrolls the strip again, and a long press picks up a tab.
- After a crash, the Mac reopened every window from the last run, and a tab could seem lost in one of them. The app now opens one window with every surviving tab in it.
- A new tab opens right after the current one.
- When a shell exited, its tab showed a "Session Ended" card as it closed, and on iPhone the bottom bar's buttons merged into blobs. The tab now just closes, and the bar fades to its new buttons.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `xros-arm64e` for a jailbroken Apple Vision Pro, or `iGhostVT-1.0.10-macos.zip` for a Mac — the Mac app is ad-hoc signed, so clear its quarantine once with `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/iGhostVT.app`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
