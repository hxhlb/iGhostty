iGhostVT 1.0.9 keeps the app responsive and its memory bounded under heavy output, remembers tab locks across launches, and lets a tab move to its own window.

- A program that printed nonstop, or retitled its tab thousands of times a second, could freeze the app for a minute or more and push its memory past a gigabyte. Output is now parsed off the main thread and retitles are paced, so ^C and the tab title answer within a second and memory settles as soon as the output stops.
- The terminal service's memory grew with every byte a session printed and never came back. Its replay buffer and the link between its two processes now stay bounded under any amount of output.
- An open tab menu greyed out and rebuilt itself while the terminal printed, so Lock was hard to hit. Menus now stay still until you pick something.
- A tab's Interaction Lock and Keyboard Lock are now kept with its session, so they survive force-quitting and relaunching the app.
- After the terminal service restarted, a reconnecting tab could attach to a session that was not its own. Session numbers now never repeat.
- A large paste into a program that was not reading its input could lose a piece from its middle without a word. A paste now arrives whole or as an unbroken beginning, and the app says when it was cut short.
- Move to New Window, in a tab's menu on iPad and Mac, takes the tab with its shell, its output and its lock into a window of its own. On iPad a tab can also be dragged out of the window. Dragging a sidebar row shows the tab instead of a blank card.
- On the Mac, a crowded tab strip scrolls instead of spilling over, and tabs and sidebar rows can be dragged to reorder again. A lone tab no longer draws a selection background. With keyboard navigation turned on, the terminal no longer wears a focus ring.
- On iPhones without a home indicator, such as iPhone SE, the bottom bar no longer touches the screen edge, and the tab switcher shows two columns.
- Tapping below the prompt to bring up the keyboard no longer pushes the top lines into scrollback (libghostty-spm 1.6.20261003).

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `xros-arm64e` for a jailbroken Apple Vision Pro, or `iGhostVT-1.0.9-macos.zip` for a Mac — the Mac app is ad-hoc signed, so clear its quarantine once with `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/iGhostVT.app`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
