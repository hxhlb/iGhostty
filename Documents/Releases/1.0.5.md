iGhostVT 1.0.5 lets its daemons read the system's protected folders.

- The daemons now carry the same data-vault storage entitlements as Fila's, so folders such as `/var/mobile/Library/Photos` no longer fail with "Operation not permitted" when the daemon opens them. Programs you run in the shell carry their own signatures and are unchanged.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `xros-arm64e` for a jailbroken Apple Vision Pro, or `iGhostVT-1.0.5-macos.zip` for a Mac — the Mac app is ad-hoc signed, so clear its quarantine once with `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/iGhostVT.app`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
