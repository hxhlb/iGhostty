iGhostVT 1.4.4 reaches a paired device on the same network directly instead of through the relay, asks about a relay file in Settings, and can open without starting a session; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- Once a paired device had been reached through the relay, iGhostVT kept going through the relay while the app was open, even with the device back on the same network. It now connects directly whenever the device is on the local network, and tries the relay first only after a direct connection has failed.
- Opening a `.vtrpsc` file asked its question on top of the terminal, which dismissed a question a tab was already asking, such as a program's request to read the clipboard. It now opens Settings ▸ Remote Access and asks there, after any question already on screen is answered. On the Mac, the Settings window opens on Remote.
- Every launch started a new session, even when you only opened the app to reach terminals that were already running. Settings ▸ Sessions now has New Session at Launch, on by default; with it off, iGhostVT opens with no tabs when there is nothing to resume.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, or `iGhostVT-1.4.4-macos.zip` for a Mac. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
