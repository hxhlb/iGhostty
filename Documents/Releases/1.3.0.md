iGhostVT 1.3.0 opens the terminals of your other devices on the same network, sends and receives files with `rz` and `sz`, and lets you choose the accent colour.

- A terminal on another device could only be reached over ssh. Turn on Settings ▸ Remote Access on a Mac, iPhone, or iPad and pair your other devices with a six-digit code: each paired device's terminals appear in the New Tab menu under its name, and a terminal opened from another device shows up as a tab on the device it runs on. Only devices found on the local network can pair, and turning the switch off disconnects every one of them.
- A terminal open on two devices had no owner. It now belongs to one device at a time: the other shows where it is in use, and Use Here brings it back. Closing a remote tab asks whether to keep the terminal running. A remote tab that loses the network reconnects by itself when the network returns.
- Files moved in and out of a terminal only by drag and drop or pasted paths. Turn on Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ ZMODEM File Transfer and `sz` saves a file from the shell to the device, and `rz` sends one from the device to the shell.
- The interface was always tinted green. Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Accent Color offers macOS's accent colours, on the Mac as well as iPhone and iPad.
- On the Mac, Settings has a Remote pane with tables of devices, and Advanced lists the two log files with a button that opens each one. The configuration file's header stays in place while its lines scroll.
- The terminal helper ran all the time. It now starts when the app needs it and exits on its own once no terminal is open and the app is closed, on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
- Apple Vision Pro is no longer supported, and there is no `xros` package.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, or `iGhostVT-1.3.0-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
