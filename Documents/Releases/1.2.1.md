iGhostVT 1.2.1 fixes the Mac's settings window and which window gets the keyboard, and redraws the Live Activity's ghost.

- On the Mac, the Settings window was a shade of grey no other app's settings use. It now has the system's window background, white in light mode and dark grey in dark.
- On the Mac, a window behind the one being typed in could take the keyboard — when its shell exited, it reconnected, or a program in it asked for the clipboard — and Return could then answer that window's clipboard prompt. Only the frontmost window takes the keyboard now, and a window gets it back as it comes to the front.
- The Live Activity's ghost is drawn in the new icon's style, with a variant for the Dynamic Island's black.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `xros-arm64e` for a jailbroken Apple Vision Pro, or `iGhostVT-1.2.1-macos.zip` for a Mac — the Mac app is ad-hoc signed, so clear its quarantine once with `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/iGhostVT.app`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
