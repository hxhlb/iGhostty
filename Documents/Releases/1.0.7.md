iGhostVT 1.0.7 moves to a newer Ghostty, so an iPad's hardware keyboard can switch input languages inside the terminal.

- On an iPad with a hardware keyboard, Caps Lock and the language key did nothing while the terminal had focus, so you could not switch to Chinese, Japanese or another input source there. They now switch input sources as they do everywhere else.
- Double-clicking to select a word selected past the end of a line, or stopped partway through wide characters such as CJK text. A word selection now ends at a hard line break and includes whole wide characters.
- With reverse wrap on, moving the cursor back past the start of a line could send it to the wrong place. It now lands on the end of the line above.
- An OSC sequence a program starts and then cancels (with CAN or SUB) no longer swallows the output that follows it.
- Kitty graphics images, `tmux` mouse handling and `tmux list-windows` have fixes from upstream Ghostty.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `xros-arm64e` for a jailbroken Apple Vision Pro, or `iGhostVT-1.0.7-macos.zip` for a Mac — the Mac app is ad-hoc signed, so clear its quarantine once with `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/iGhostVT.app`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
