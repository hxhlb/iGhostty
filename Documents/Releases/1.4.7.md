iGhostVT 1.4.7 adds Ghost Remote, which opens terminals on your iGhostVT devices from an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, and shows a failed pairing as an alert; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- Opening a terminal on one of your devices from an iPhone or iPad meant running iGhostVT on it, which needs custom firmware. Ghost Remote is iGhostVT's remote access on its own: install `GhostRemote-1.4.7.ipa` with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly, or add the AltStore source `https://owngoal-dev.github.io/iGhostVT/altstore.json`, then tap + and pair with the code your device shows. It connects over the local network or through your relay, reopens your tabs after iOS closes it in the background, and talks to devices on any 1.4 release.
- A wrong pairing code, or a device not open for pairing, showed a line of red text under the code field that was easy to miss. It is now an alert, and the field is ready for the next code once you dismiss it.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.7-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.7.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
