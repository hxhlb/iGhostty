iGhostVT 1.4.2 lists a paired device's open terminals before its recent directories in New Tab; it connects to devices on 1.4.0 and 1.4.1 as well.

- In New Tab, a paired device's submenu put the directories you had used there above the terminals it has open. It now follows the order of this device's own rows: New Terminal, then the open terminals, then Recent.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, or `iGhostVT-1.4.2-macos.zip` for a Mac. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
