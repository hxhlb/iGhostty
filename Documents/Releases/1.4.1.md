iGhostVT 1.4.1 opens the New Tab menu at once and fixes pairing on the Mac; it connects to devices on 1.4.0 as well.

- New Tab showed Loading… until every paired device had answered, which through a relay could take a while. The menu now opens at once; a device's open terminals appear in its submenu as soon as that device answers.
- On the Mac, the code you typed while pairing was white on a light field, and the Done button's title was dark on the accent colour. Both now use the system's colours.
- On the Mac, a device you had just paired stayed under Other Devices with its Pair button until the list was opened again. It now moves to the paired devices as soon as pairing finishes.
- Devices on different patch releases, such as 1.4.0 and 1.4.1, now connect to each other. Only a different minor or major version, such as 1.5, still asks you to update.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, or `iGhostVT-1.4.1-macos.zip` for a Mac. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
