iGhostVT 1.3.2 copies files you drop on a remote tab to the device the terminal runs on, and makes the New Tab menu's other devices quicker to reach.

- Dropping a file or a screenshot on a tab whose terminal runs on another device pasted a path from this device, which that terminal could not open. The file is now copied to the other device first, with a progress bar and a button to cancel, and the path there is pasted. If the network drops during the copy, it reconnects and continues from where it stopped. Folders are not copied.
- The New Tab menu showed each paired device as a submenu, even when there was only one. With one or two devices, each device's terminals are now listed directly under its name; with three or more, each device still has its own submenu.
- New Tab only offered recent folders on this device. It now also lists the folders your tabs recently used on each paired device, and choosing one opens a terminal there in that folder.
- On the Mac, File ▸ New Tab on Device could keep saying No Paired Devices Nearby after a device was found, or keep listing terminals that had been closed. The menu now updates when devices and their terminals change.
- Remote Access could remember a device's IPv6 address, which is hard to read and stops working when it changes. It now remembers the device's IPv4 address.
- If the host's remote-access helper quit unexpectedly, remote access stayed off until the device restarted. The helper now restarts on its own.
- The accent colour row in Settings had extra space at its left and right edges. The colours now line up with the edges of the row.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, or `iGhostVT-1.3.2-macos.zip` for a Mac. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
