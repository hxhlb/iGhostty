iGhostVT 1.2.0 has a new icon, gives the Mac a settings window of its own, and tidies Settings, the lock badge, and the Live Activity on iPhone and iPad.

- A new app icon: a glass ghost whose eyes are two block cursors, on a white tile in light mode and graphite in dark.
- On the Mac, Settings opened as a card over the terminal. It now opens in its own window, with General, Appearance, Keyboard, Configuration, Advanced, and About along the toolbar, and the window fits each pane. Advanced opens the app's and the helper's logs in Console.
- In Settings on iPhone and iPad, the row icons were misaligned and tinted differently, and every page ended in a large empty gap that cut off the last section's corners. The icons and the gap are gone. Default Shell, Keep Sessions Running, and Recent Directories moved from Advanced to the main page, and the Ghostty configuration has a page of its own.
- In the theme list, rows kept an empty column for the checkmark and their separators stopped short. Rows now span the width, and the selected theme's swatch carries the checkmark. Licenses show Ghostty's commit as seven characters instead of forty.
- A locked tab showed a "Locked" label over the top of the terminal for as long as it stayed locked. The label now fades after a moment, and the padlock stays in the tab's title on iPhone as it does everywhere else.
- The Live Activity spent most of its space on a large count and a progress bar. It now shows the session counts on one line and each session's running program and directory below.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `xros-arm64e` for a jailbroken Apple Vision Pro, or `iGhostVT-1.2.0-macos.zip` for a Mac — the Mac app is ad-hoc signed, so clear its quarantine once with `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/iGhostVT.app`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
