iGhostVT 1.0.6 is checked to launch on iOS 15 before it is published; the app itself is unchanged.

- Every iOS package is now inspected before it is packaged for anything the oldest supported iOS does not have. A library update of that kind made another app quit the moment it opened on iOS 26; from this release on, a package with such a problem is never published. Nothing you see or use in iGhostVT has changed.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `xros-arm64e` for a jailbroken Apple Vision Pro, or `iGhostVT-1.0.6-macos.zip` for a Mac — the Mac app is ad-hoc signed, so clear its quarantine once with `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/iGhostVT.app`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
