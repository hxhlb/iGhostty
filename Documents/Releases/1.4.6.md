iGhostVT 1.4.6 updates its terminal library and changes nothing you use; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- The terminal library is now libghostty-spm 2.2.2026100703. Its one fix, a background color that stayed light in dark mode, never showed in iGhostVT, so terminals look and behave as they did in 1.4.5.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, or `iGhostVT-1.4.6-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
